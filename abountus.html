Our History

Deez Entertainment SL is one of the most influential and prolific entertainment organizations in Sierra Leone. Our mission is to build confidence and promote the younger generation toward a brighter and better future.

We aim to unite Sierra Leoneans through love, peace, and unity while producing reality TV shows, music and movie productions, event promotions, and creative collaborations across the country and beyond.

DEEZ FOR THE GEEZ — STRICTLY FOR THE GEEZ.

Biography of the CEO

Mr. Mohammed Kargbo is a dedicated Sierra Leonean entrepreneur actively involved in show business and global collaborations. He attended Albert Academy Senior Secondary School and later studied Social Science at Njala University, Bo City.

As the Founder and CEO of Deez Entertainment SL, he has successfully organized numerous sporting, entertainment, and community-based events. His vision and leadership continue to push Deez Entertainment SL to greater heights within the entertainment industry.
